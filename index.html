Chronological Timeline (Documented Events per Era)

Gap Detection — Under-Documented Periods

  • Egypt — Intermediate Periods: Multiple transitional dynasties remain poorly documented due to limited surviving records.
  • China — Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms (907–960 CE): Fragmented rule resulted in sparse cross-regional historical accounts.
  • India — Post-Gupta Era (550–700 CE): Regional fragmentation led to inconsistent record-keeping across kingdoms.
  • Greece — Greek Dark Ages (1100–800 BCE): Collapse of Bronze Age palace culture created a gap in written records.
  • Italy — Migration Period (376–568 CE): Disruption of Roman administration produced incomplete administrative records.
  • Mexico — Pre-Classic to Classic transition (~250 CE): Limited surviving codices leave aspects of early Maya history unclear.
