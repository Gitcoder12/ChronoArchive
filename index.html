Egypt

Timeline: Ancient → Ptolemaic → Roman → Islamic → Modern

  • Museums: Egyptian Museum (Cairo), Grand Egyptian Museum
  • Notable Artifacts: Rosetta Stone (British Museum), Tutankhamun's mask
  • Key Periods: Old Kingdom (~2686 BCE), New Kingdom (~1550 BCE), Ptolemaic Dynasty (323–30 BCE)
  • Gap Detection: Under-documented transitional periods between dynasties
×

Greece

Timeline: Minoan → Mycenaean → Classical → Hellenistic → Byzantine → Modern

  • Museums: National Archaeological Museum (Athens), Acropolis Museum
  • Notable Artifacts: Elgin Marbles (British Museum), Antikythera Mechanism
  • Key Periods: Classical Greece (~500–323 BCE), Hellenistic Era (323–31 BCE)
  • Gap Detection: Greek Dark Ages (~1100–800 BCE) remain sparsely documented
×

China

Timeline: Shang → Zhou → Qin → Han → Tang → Song → Ming → Qing → Republic → Modern

  • Museums: Palace Museum (Forbidden City), National Museum of China
  • Notable Artifacts: Terracotta Army, Bronze Vessels of the Shang Dynasty
  • Key Periods: Qin unification (221 BCE), Tang golden age (618–907 CE), Mongol Yuan dynasty (1271–1368)
  • Gap Detection: Limited records survive from pre-Shang periods
×

India

Timeline: Indus Valley → Vedic → Maurya → Gupta → Mughal → Colonial → Modern

  • Museums: National Museum (New Delhi), Indian Museum (Kolkata)
  • Notable Artifacts: Ashoka Pillar, Ellora cave sculptures
  • Key Periods: Indus Valley (~2500 BCE), Maurya Empire (322–185 BCE), Mughal Empire (1526–1857)
  • Gap Detection: Post-Indus collapse period (~1900–1500 BCE) is poorly understood
×

Italy

Timeline: Etruscan → Roman Republic → Roman Empire → Medieval → Renaissance → Modern

  • Museums: Vatican Museums, Uffizi Gallery (Florence), Colosseum
  • Notable Artifacts: Laocoön Group, Capitoline Wolf, Venus de Milo (origin debated)
  • Key Periods: Roman Republic (509–27 BCE), Roman Empire (27 BCE–476 CE), Renaissance (14th–17th century)
  • Gap Detection: Early medieval Italy (476–800 CE) has significant documentation gaps
×

France

Timeline: Gauls → Roman Gaul → Frankish Kingdom → Medieval → Revolution → Republic → Modern

  • Museums: Louvre Museum, Musée d'Orsay, Palace of Versailles
  • Notable Artifacts: Winged Victory of Samothrace, Code of Hammurabi (Louvre)
  • Key Periods: Carolingian Empire (800 CE), French Revolution (1789), Napoleonic Era (1799–1815)
  • Gap Detection: Merovingian dynasty records are fragmentary
×

United Kingdom

Timeline: Prehistoric → Roman Britain → Anglo-Saxon → Norman → Tudor → Empire → Modern

  • Museums: British Museum, Victoria and Albert Museum, National Museum of Scotland
  • Notable Artifacts: Sutton Hoo Helmet, Lindow Man, Magna Carta
  • Key Periods: Norman Conquest (1066), Elizabethan Era (1558–1603), British Empire peak (19th century)
  • Gap Detection: Pre-Roman Celtic oral traditions are largely undocumented in writing
×

Japan

Timeline: Jōmon → Yayoi → Kofun → Nara → Heian → Feudal → Edo → Meiji → Modern

  • Museums: Tokyo National Museum, Kyoto National Museum
  • Notable Artifacts: Dotaku bronze bells, Haniwa terracotta figures
  • Key Periods: Heian Era (794–1185 CE), Edo Period (1603–1868), Meiji Restoration (1868)
  • Gap Detection: Jōmon to Yayoi transition is an active area of archaeological research
×

Iraq (Mesopotamia)

Timeline: Ubaid → Sumerian → Akkadian → Babylonian → Assyrian → Persian → Islamic → Modern

  • Museums: Iraq Museum (Baghdad), British Museum (Mesopotamian collection)
  • Notable Artifacts: Standard of Ur, Ishtar Gate, Epic of Gilgamesh tablets
  • Key Periods: Sumerian city-states (~3500 BCE), Babylonian Empire (~1894–539 BCE), Assyrian Empire (~900–612 BCE)
  • Gap Detection: Many cuneiform records remain untranslated or lost to looting
×

Mexico

Timeline: Olmec → Maya → Teotihuacan → Toltec → Aztec → Colonial → Modern

  • Museums: National Museum of Anthropology (Mexico City), Templo Mayor Museum
  • Notable Artifacts: Aztec Sun Stone, Olmec Colossal Heads
  • Key Periods: Olmec (~1500–400 BCE), Teotihuacan (~1–650 CE), Aztec Empire (1428–1521)
  • Gap Detection: The collapse of Teotihuacan (~650 CE) is not fully explained
×

Peru

Timeline: Norte Chico → Chavín → Moche → Wari → Tiwanaku → Inca → Colonial → Modern

  • Museums: Larco Museum (Lima), Machu Picchu Site Museum
  • Notable Artifacts: Nazca Lines, Inca gold figurines, quipus (knotted records)
  • Key Periods: Norte Chico civilisation (~3000 BCE), Inca Empire (1438–1533 CE)
  • Gap Detection: Intermediate Period (~600–900 CE) is underrepresented in records
×

Turkey

Timeline: Hittite → Phrygian → Lydian → Persian → Hellenistic → Roman → Byzantine → Ottoman → Modern

  • Museums: Topkapi Palace Museum, Museum of Anatolian Civilizations (Ankara)
  • Notable Artifacts: Hittite lion sculptures, Byzantine mosaics of Hagia Sophia
  • Key Periods: Hittite Empire (~1600–1180 BCE), Byzantine Empire (330–1453 CE), Ottoman Empire (1299–1922)
  • Gap Detection: The post-Hittite collapse period (~1180–900 BCE) has significant gaps
